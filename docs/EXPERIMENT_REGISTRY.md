# Experiment registry

Keep negative outcomes. They are research evidence, not messages to Telegram.

Existing fixed studies are preserved in `SOURCED_RESEARCH.md`,
`MEAN_REVERSION_RESEARCH.md`, `INTRADAY_RESEARCH.md`, `CURRENT_RESEARCH.md`,
`EDGE_SEARCH.md`, `INDEPENDENT_RESULTS.md` and `CRYPTO_RESULTS.md`.
Previously inspected observations cannot become a new blind sample.

New study records will contain the registered family/grid, source and parameter
hashes, chronological split, trial count, train-only shortlist, validation lock,
final result, cost stress, uncertainty and the explicit qualification decision.
The broad search and image-inspired liquidity hypotheses are separate studies;
their outcomes must not be pooled selectively to manufacture a winner.

An interrupted or blocked experiment is recorded as such, never as a passing
test. Raw downloaded candles stay outside application distributions.
