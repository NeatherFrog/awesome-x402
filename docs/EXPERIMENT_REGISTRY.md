## Statistical and correlation analysis

Hourly/portfolio studies use causal next-open execution, training-only family
shortlists/regime features, costed baselines, daily-result correlations and
conditional circular7-day block resampling. Broad training mean pairwise strategy
correlation was0.4348: repeated indicators do not provide independent evidence.
Holm-adjusted validation tests and99% confidence gates did not admit a primary.
Funding uses daily total cash-account returns including flat days, real settlement
rates, equal spot/perpetual quantities, finite isolated margin and9 frozen base/
stress runs for the final construction. Funding settlements are not independent
trades. See[STATISTICAL_PROTOCOL.md](STATISTICAL_PROTOCOL.md).

Block inference is conditional on the observed distribution/approximate
stationarity; symmetry/independent blocks and future market regimes are not
proved. Sequential adaptive choices and earlier inspected overlapping2026 prices
remain disclosed. No global untouched-final claim applies. Full FVG reports later
variants descriptively; these final observations are already consumed for any
future selection. Macro/sentiment/news inputs were not fabricated or declared
causally profitable without timestamped data.
