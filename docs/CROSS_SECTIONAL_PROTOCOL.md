# Preregistered current-survivor cross-sectional crypto study

This protocol is fixed before strategy outcomes. It is a new adaptation of the original Liu/Tsyvinski/Wu cryptocurrency-factor paper, not a replication of its returns. The original paper, documentary FTMO mapping, acquisition receipts, and design choices are described in `docs/CROSS_SECTIONAL_DESIGN.md` and bound by hashes in the machine protocol. All earlier studied strategies and reused market periods remain adaptive selection history.

## Scope and catalogue

The fixed cohort is BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT, XRPUSDT, ADAUSDT, DOGEUSDT, LINKUSDT, LTCUSDT, BNBUSDT, BCHUSDT, and DOTUSDT. The eleven current survivors all have checked December 2023 futures observations and map to today's FTMO catalogue. This is explicitly survivor-conditioned; it does not establish the pre-2024 historical FTMO roster, cover delisted competitors, or estimate an unbiased cryptocurrency universe.

There are exactly **72 configurations**: three families × 7/14/28-day momentum × daily/seven-day rebalance × raw/volatility-normalized rank × 0.5%/1.0% per-position entry stop-risk caps. All other choices below are fixed. No new variant, threshold, risk scaling, cohort replacement, or alternative primary is introduced after results.

Families are long relative strength, initially dollar-balanced momentum with a retained book, and initially dollar-balanced momentum with an observed-financing adjustment. Balanced families project equal long/short dollars after the individual risk and asset caps: trim larger-side new quantities, then proportionally trim larger-side retained positions if required. Quantity rounding creates a residual bounded by the declared lot quantum. Actual fills can be incomplete and the book can drift or lose a leg within an hour; record actual long/short exposure and do not claim atomic execution or beta neutrality. At the next known opening, proportional reductions restore the planned balance without spending cash from unverified same-hour exits.

## Causal signals

Sources are official checksum-verified USD-M trade1h, calculated mark1h, and realized funding. Each hourly row is labelled by its UTC open; its complete OHLC/volume becomes available at open+1h. December 2023 is warm-up. TRAIN is the complete 2024 calendar, fresh $100,000 with no inherited positions or evaluation profits.

For decision day `d`, momentum is `log(close[d-2]/close[d-2-L])`, skipping the newest complete day. Volatility is the sample standard deviation of the prior 28 complete daily log returns, floored at 0.005 for normalization and inverse-volatility weights. ATR20 is Wilder daily ATR from completed traded-price observations. Historical placeholder extrema can affect ATR provisionally; an absence of trades never creates a fill. Require actual traded final hourly closes for each of the prior 30 feature days, median prior-30-day USDT quote volume at least $20 million, and complete features. All liquidity and volatility inputs are prior to the decision.

Long strength holds the three highest-ranked eligible assets with positive absolute price momentum; unfilled slots remain cash. Balanced families require at least six eligible assets, long the top three and short the bottom three, with disjoint names. Absolute price momentum need not be negative for a short. Financing-adjusted rank is `M/L - sum(realized funding rates known during the prior seven calendar days)/7`; the optional normalization divides that daily score by daily volatility. This persistence estimate is a hypothesis, never an observation of future funding.

Signals are completed at UTC midnight. Initial market-entry intents are considered at the next 01:00 hourly opening. Seven-day rebalances are anchored to 2024-01-01 and retain that anchor in later stages. Intents expire two hours after the first intended opening, permitting funds from a previous hour's completed close to be used later. Same-side selected positions retain their entry/stop and base quantity except for defensive risk or side-balance reductions. They are not artificially closed and reopened merely to count turnover. A stopped asset remains blocked until the next registered rebalance.

## Capital and executable-price interpretation

Initial TOTAL capital is $100,000. Each position reserves its full modeled entry notional as 1x isolated collateral plus entry commission, from one positive free-cash wallet. Entry gross is at most `min(initial,current_marked_equity)` and each asset at most 0.35 of that ceiling. The lower-risk catalogue caps aggregate planned stop risk at 3% of total current equity; the higher-risk catalogue at 4%. Stops are two completed daily ATR20 units from the modeled entry. Unit stop risk includes entry fee, adverse exit movement, and side-dependent exit-fill fee. Base-unit step 0.001, maintenance 0.5%, and liquidation fee 0.5% are explicit proxy assumptions, not verified historical FTMO filters or tiers.

All quantities, side balancing, cash reservations, and portfolio risk reductions use the same prior-known opening account state before checking any exact-boundary funding outcome, current whole-hour volume, current old first-trade price, current calculated mark, or recovering an old closing wallet. Old gross, stop risk and retained-side balancing use preceding completed calculated marks. An unfilled or late old exit cannot enlarge another asset's order during that hour. Retrospective actual old first prints plus entered new positions are checked for possible union gross and stop-risk breaches; any breach fails base/stress qualification, without resizing or dropping the locked orders. No extra capital, hidden credit, free spot asset, cross-margin transfer, or immediately spendable unrealized profit is introduced.

Commission is **0.065% per side** from the current FTMO catalogue, deliberately using the more conservative interpretation of its undocumented side convention. Add 2 basis points adverse slippage and half of a 1-basis-point full spread per side. Double commission, slippage, and spread together in stress. This is 9 modeled basis points per side before financing. The current FTMO Swing crypto leverage is 1; its actual swap, execution, sessions, quantity rules, and historical eligibility differ from this Binance perpetual surrogate.

Trade prices provide modeled fills; calculated marks never do. Each mark source retains its auxiliary count without calling it executed trade volume. Known prior marks size the opening wallet; current mark OPEN and adverse bounds are used only afterward for conservative automatic-liquidation/risk interpretation. An old opening mark-gap breach cannot recover a healthy wallet using a later traded price. After a possible late debit, recheck maintenance before recovering a closing wallet. A latched liquidation forfeits the modeled wallet; later recovery cannot restore it. Any liquidation or unfunded deficit fails qualification.

Trade-volume-zero bars cannot fill an entry or a pending exit. Held absence of trades, uncertain reopening exits, mark count at most two on any held old/new hourly exposure, and sparse prior known marks used by opening plans have explicit counters and fail the historical screen. A complete file calendar is not a proof of a dense observed risk path. No future volume chooses another order's quantity.

Partial reductions allocate original margin, accrued funding, and entry fee proportionally, round the retained quantity down, and pay the actual modeled exit cost. Closed slices retain one asset episode ID. The total account identity is reconciled from cash, original contributed margins, marked open PnL, paid costs, settlements, closed slices, and any explicitly reported liquidation adjustment. Every base and stress ledger is retained, including failed configurations.

## Funding, intrahour risk, and boundaries

Funding uses actual settlement timestamp milliseconds and decimal rates, with no assumed universal eight-hour schedule. This source window happens to contain eight-hour recorded intervals; that observation does not determine later schedules. Events become known at settlement. Actual rates cannot resize earlier plans or finance their earlier cash reservation.

A source-hour fill's precise second is unverified. Possible debit after a modeled entry is retained; uncertain positive income requires a fully older position and no exact-time, stop, exit, or liquidation ambiguity. Exact-time positive credit is omitted. Funding uses the preceding completed mark for exact boundaries and the computed mark-hour open otherwise; neither proves the exact settlement mark. Possible late-hour debit on an old exiting/reduced full position is retained once, uncertain credit omitted, then allocated with the reduction. A last-processed timestamp prevents double collection on the retained piece. These are conservative bounds, not exact exchange account receipts or FTMO swaps.

An opening label is the first trade somewhere in that hour. Old exits therefore use the traded open as a fill proxy and carry the hour-end known-at as an upper time bound. Record that clock distinction. Stops use pessimistic traded extreme/gap handling, and their exact intrahour exit second remains unknown. Calculated mark high/low bounds cannot reveal execution order. Daily favorable/adverse envelopes include the union of old positions awaiting their first exit trade and new positions that might fill earlier in the same hour, on the original physical cash with new margin/fees deducted. Unchanged positions are counted once. Favorable-before-adverse ordering and combined cross-asset extrema are conservative risk bounds, not a realized path.

The sample-boundary shutdown is predeclared at the final source hour's traded open, with completion known by that hour's close. This gives an observable trade within the scored source window rather than inventing a next-month opening/funding mark. A missing final-hour trade leaves an unresolved open position and fails. A completed prior-hour exit cannot receive funding from the next source month.
